My research focuses on **learning with limited supervision** and **transferring knowledge across modalities**, with applications in computer vision and 3D perception. I work on semi-supervised and self-supervised learning, as well as cross-modal knowledge distillation.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from [POSTECH](https://cse.postech.ac.kr/) in 2026, advised by [Prof. Suha Kwak](https://suhakwak.github.io/) in the [Computer Vision Lab](https://cvlab.postech.ac.kr/).
